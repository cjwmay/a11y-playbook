Day 1 · 对比度 4.5:1Contrast 4.5:1 — don't let gray body text get too light

WCAG 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) · AA

📏 规则一句话The rule in one sentence

中文
正文小字和背景的对比度至少 4.5:1，大字（18pt 以上 / 14pt 加粗以上）至少 3:1 —— 灰色越淡，低视力用户和强光下的手机用户越读不了。
ENGLISH
Body text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background; large text (18pt+ / 14pt bold+) needs at least 3:1. The lighter your gray, the more low-vision users and phone users in sunlight can't read it.

❌ 反例：#999 的"次要信息"Bad: #999 "secondary" text

2.85:1
AA 小字 FAILAA 大字 FAIL

设计师常拿淡灰表达"次要"，但 2.85:1 在阳光下几乎隐形。
Designers love light gray for "secondary" — at 2.85:1 it's nearly invisible in sunlight.

✅ 正例：#595959，深但不黑Good: #595959, dark but not black

预计送达时间：3–5 个工作日

7.00:1
AA 小字 PASSAAA PASS

层级用字重（Regular→Medium）区分，不用"更淡"。
Express hierarchy with weight, not lightness.

💡 临界值色号：#767676 在白色上正好 4.54:1 —— 比它淡的灰，小字一律不过线。记住它，当 Figma 里的"最淡可用灰"。
Threshold swatch: #767676 is exactly 4.54:1 on white — any lighter gray fails for body text. Memorize it as your "lightest usable gray".

🔍 怎么测How to test

中文
  1. Chrome DevTools：选中文字 → Styles 面板点颜色方块 → 取色器直接显示 contrast ratio 和 AA/AAA 徽章。
  2. Figma：装 Stark 或 A11y Annotation 插件，一键扫全画面的对比度。
  3. 在线：WebAIM Contrast Checker，输入两个色号出比值。
ENGLISH
  1. Chrome DevTools: select the text → click the color swatch in the Styles panel → the picker shows the ratio and AA/AAA badges.
  2. Figma: Stark or A11y Annotation plugin scans the whole frame.
  3. Online: WebAIM Contrast Checker — paste two hex values.

🛠️ 一行修复The one-line fix

/* 次要文字：别再用 #999 / secondary text: stop using #999 */
color: #999999; color: #595959; /* 2.85:1 → 7.0:1 */
font-weight: 500; /* 层级用字重，不用更淡 / hierarchy via weight, not lightness */
🎙️ 面试怎么讲："我在 DS 里把 secondary text 的 token 从 #999 换成 #595959，对比度从 2.85 拉到 7.0，同时用字重而不是颜色深浅表达层级 —— 这是 WCAG 1.4.3 的 AA 要求。"
🎙️ Interview line: "I changed our secondary-text token from #999 to #595959, taking contrast from 2.85 to 7.0, and expressed hierarchy with weight instead of lightness — that's WCAG 1.4.3 AA."
